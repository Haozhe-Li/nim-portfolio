# Meet Rix: A Versatile Model Built for Omni's Agentic Tasks

Starting today, all users will be able to select our latest and first-ever in-house model: Rix, purpose-built for answer quality, factual grounding, and a user-friendly response style. Rix is built on top of Qwen3-30B-A3B, and we've further trained it to push its capabilities closer to the frontier. As a mixture-of-experts model with only 3 billion activated parameters, Rix delivers performance close to frontier-class models — including proprietary ones — at a fraction of the cost.

## Rix Is Fast

Rix is the fastest model in our benchmark, responding in just 6.6s — 1.4x faster than GPT-5.6-Luna (9.3s) and 2.6x faster than Gemini 3.6 Flash (17.4s). Running on our own general-purpose GPU clusters rather than specialized inference hardware, that speed advantage comes purely from how Rix is built and served.

## Rix Excels at Providing High-Quality Answers

We optimized Rix along two dimensions:

- **Factuality:** how well an answer is grounded in verifiable information
- **Readability:** how clearly and helpfully an answer is formatted for the user

Compared with openai/gpt-oss-120b — a substantially larger and heavier model — Rix performs exceptionally well on both dimensions.
